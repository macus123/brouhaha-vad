TEMPORAL ORDER PRESERVATION:
============================
The algorithm processes segments in chronological order from the original audio.

Speech segments are kept whole to preserve context, causing some discontinuities.
Non-speech segments may be split to meet duration targets.
Detailed jump analysis is available in the individual timestamp files.
